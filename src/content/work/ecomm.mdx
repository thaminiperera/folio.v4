## Problem

I needed a commerce surface I could reason about: products you can find, a cart that stays honest, and login that does not invent a backend fairy tale.

## What I built

Ecomm is a React storefront on Material UI. People search by title, filter by category and price, and move items through a cart with quantities. Login and registration use Formik and Yup. Auth state lives in context and persists in `localStorage`. Routing covers dashboard, cart, and account pages, with toast feedback for the usual successes and failures.

It is a front-end system with clear seams — cards, cart, forms — not a payments product. Session expiry, loading states, and a cart badge were left as obvious next cuts.

## Stack

React, Material UI, Formik, Yup, React Router.
