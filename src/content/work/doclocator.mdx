## Problem

Finding a doctor who is actually nearby, available, and willing to take an on-site request is still mostly word of mouth. The product needed two doors — patients and clinicians — and a map that did not lie.

## What I built

DocLocator was internship work at Archnix. I designed and developed the **doctor portal**: sign-in, a dashboard of pending visits, and the ability to accept or cancel. Patients book by specialty, availability, and location. Doctors inside a 20 km radius are filtered with the Haversine formula; Google Maps draws both sides. Cancellations notify the patient.

The server is Node and Express on MySQL, with parameterized queries. The client is React.

## Stack

React, Node.js, Express, MySQL, Google Maps API.
