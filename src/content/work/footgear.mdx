## Problem

Online shoe shopping guesses. Size charts lie; returns pile up. We wanted a try-on you can see, and a measurement that does not require a shop assistant.

## What I built

Footgear is a Flutter app with a product list and a Snap AR overlay: a 3D shoe on the live camera. Beside that, a Python service takes a photo of a foot on A4 paper, preprocesses it, segments with k-means, finds edges and a bounding box, and returns a length in centimetres. Images can travel as base64. The vision side was meant to sit on EC2; the interesting design is the pipeline, not the host.

I worked in a small team. The measurement module is the part I can speak to in detail.

## Stack

Flutter and Snap AR on device. Python, OpenCV, and scikit-learn for size.
